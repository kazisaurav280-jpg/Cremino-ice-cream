Our Story

Cremino started in 2019 as a small kitchen experiment in Dhanmondi, Dhaka. Our founder, Jenny Wilson, wanted ice cream that tasted of real ingredients, not syrups and stabilisers. She began churning small batches with fresh milk from local dairy farms, seasonal mangoes from Rajshahi and chocolate with an ingredient list you could actually read. Friends asked for more, then their friends did too. Today every Cremino flavor is still made in our own Dhaka kitchen, and the rule hasn't changed: if we wouldn't serve it to our own family, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

The simple joy you never outgrow.

We make every flavor in small batches, so each tub gets the attention it deserves. Our milk comes from partner dairy farms just outside Dhaka and reaches our kitchen within hours. Mangoes, litchis and strawberries are bought in season and frozen at their best, never made from concentrate.

No artificial colours, no shortcuts, and a no-added-sugar range for anyone who wants the treat without the sugar.

Pure ingredients.
Unapologetic richness.

We specialise in flavors you grew up with, made the way ice cream should be. Think creamy Himsagar mango, rich chocolate fudge, a rose-and-pistachio scoop inspired by falooda, and Malai Kulfi made the slow, traditional way. Every recipe is tested many times in our kitchen before it reaches the counter. We keep the ingredient list short, the texture silky and the price fair, so a scoop of Cremino can be an everyday treat, not just a special occasion. New seasonal flavors arrive every few months.
